# My own thoughts
* As a User Experience Researcher I am prone to use AI in my every day job 
* I am also researching on AI features every day 
* Like any new technology, it is important to adapt and to learn new things. 
* However, with AI, it´s different. You can for one of the few times ask yourself if you want to do it yourself or if you want to delegate. 
* If you want to have your own personal assistant do it. 
* We are not talking about any personal assistant. We are talking about a skilled intern to whom you potentially have to monitor a lot. 
* Additional thoughts
	* Trust - over and under trust. 
	* criticality of the task.
	* Hamburger approach. 
	* Whatever we delegate, it is something we don´t do anymore. 
	* The impact of not being able to have a conversation on a topic. 
	* The act of thinking. 
* What to do about it
	* Map your tasks
	* Do your SOP
	* What is repetitive and what is not.
